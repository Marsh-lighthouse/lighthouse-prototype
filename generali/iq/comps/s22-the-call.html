S22 · Phase 0 — The Call

Static comps for sign-off. No wiring exists behind these. The grammar is a real-time call: the interviewer is the other participant, questions are spoken with live captions, the candidate's turn takes speaker focus, and every control lives on one persistent call bar. Marsh palette throughout; copy is the real seed content. Approve, annotate, or reject — implementation begins only after sign-off.
